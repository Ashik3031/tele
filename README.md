# Tele

Tele is a modern **Real-Time Sales Management Platform** designed for businesses that need complete visibility into their sales operations. The platform enables sales teams, managers, and business leaders to monitor live sales activities, track team performance, and make faster business decisions through real-time dashboards and analytics.

## 🚀 Features

### 📊 Real-Time Dashboard
- Live sales updates
- Instant dashboard synchronization
- Real-time KPIs and metrics
- Sales performance overview

### 👥 Sales Team Management
- Manage sales executives
- Assign leads and customers
- Monitor individual performance
- Track daily activities

### 🏢 Sales Manager Panel
- View team performance
- Monitor active sales
- Analyze conversion rates
- Generate reports
- Track targets and achievements

### 🎯 Sales Head Dashboard
- Company-wide sales analytics
- Regional/team comparisons
- Revenue tracking
- Business insights
- Performance monitoring

### 📈 Live Analytics
- Sales trends
- Revenue tracking
- Target vs Achievement
- Team productivity
- Performance reports

### 🔔 Real-Time Updates
- Instant sales notifications
- Live dashboard refresh
- Real-time activity feed
- Automatic data synchronization

### 🔐 Role-Based Access
- Admin
- Sales Head
- Sales Manager
- Sales Executive

Each user has access only to the features relevant to their role.

---

## 💡 Why Tele?

Managing sales through spreadsheets or delayed reports makes it difficult to react quickly.

Tele solves this problem by providing:

- Real-time sales tracking
- Centralized sales management
- Live team monitoring
- Instant reporting
- Data-driven decision making
